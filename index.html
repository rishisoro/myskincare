Golden Rules

  • Never scrub, never use hot water, never wash more than twice a day.
  • Sunscreen every single day — this matters more for even tone than the serum.
  • Don't pick spots/bumps — picking creates the dark marks you want to remove.
  • Pat products on, don't rub hard. Toner always with palms, no cotton rubbing.

Do NOT Combine (until Week 6 re-check)

  • No Vitamin C, retinol, salicylic acid, glycolic acid, scrubs, or fragranced aftershave.
  • All your products contain niacinamide — that is why the routine is staged.

Go Back To Basics If…

  • Burning lasts more than 1–2 minutes, itching or swelling.
  • Redness is clearly worse for 2 days in a row.
  • Many new small bumps keep spreading.

Then: stop the newest product, do Stage A (Face Wash + Gel Cream + Sunscreen) for 3 days.

Shave Day Rules

  • Shave after warm Face Wash, with the grain, rinse blade often.
  • That day: skip Toner and Serum completely.
  • If still red next day, do one more basics-only day.
